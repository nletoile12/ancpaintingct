Common questions

Do you move furniture?

Yes. We move furniture to the middle of the room and cover it, and we cover floors before we start. Small, fragile or valuable items are best put away ahead of time.

Do you fix nail holes and dings?

Yes. Filling nail holes, small dings and minor drywall cracks is part of our prep. Bigger drywall repairs we'll price separately in your quote.

How much does painting cost?

Every job is different, so we give you a free written quote after a quick look. The price depends on the size of the space, how much prep is needed and how many coats it takes. No surprises later.

What paint do you use?

We use Benjamin Moore paint. If you have a color in mind, great. If not, we're happy to help you pick.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size of the job. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote, and then we stick to it.

Ready for a fresh coat?

Takes 30 seconds. We'll text you two times that work, then give you a clear written quote.
