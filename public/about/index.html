ANC Painting is a small, family-owned painting company based in Coventry, Connecticut. Nick runs the jobs himself with a small crew, so the person who gives you the quote is the person who makes sure it's done right.

We don't do high-pressure sales. We come out, look at the job, give you a clear written price, and let you decide. If you hire us, we show up when we say, protect your home, use Benjamin Moore paint, and leave the place cleaner than we found it.

Nick also runs Oil Me Up!, a mobile oil change service around Hartford, so a lot of our neighbors already know us.
