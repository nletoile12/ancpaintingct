Common questions

How much does painting cost?

Every job is different, so we give you a free written quote after a quick look. The price depends on the size of the space, how much prep is needed and how many coats it takes. No surprises later.

What paint do you use?

We use Benjamin Moore paint. If you have a color in mind, great. If not, we're happy to help you pick.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size of the job. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote, and then we stick to it.

Do I have to be home?

For the quote visit, it helps if you're there so we can talk through what you want. During the work, you don't need to be home.
